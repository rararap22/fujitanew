FAQs

Find answers to common questions about Fujita’s products, services, and technologies.
We're here to provide clarity and support for all your inquiries

What types of elevators does Fujita offer?

Fujita offers a wide range of elevators, including passenger, residential, observation, hospital, freight, automobile, and dumbwaiter elevators.

Is Fujita suitable for small or limited spaces?

Yes. Our Flexi Lift and wheelchair lift are specifically designed for buildings with limited shaft space or compact layouts.

How energy-efficient are Fujita elevators?

Fujita elevators are built with VVVF drive systems and energy-saving technologies, consuming as little as 800 watts in some models.

Does Fujita provide installation services?

Yes. Fujita offers complete services from consultation and design to installation and after-sales support.

Can Fujita elevators be customized?

Absolutely. We offer customized cabin designs, door configurations, and technical specifications based on your building’s needs.

What safety features are included?

Fujita elevators include advanced safety systems such as emergency stop buttons, overload sensors, and smooth braking mechanisms.

How can I request maintenance or service?

You can contact our service center directly via phone, email, or the “Contact” page on our website to schedule maintenance.

Where are Fujita products manufactured?

Fujita elevators are built using Excellent Technology, with strict quality control and certified international standards.
